Fort Pierce, honestly described.

Fort Pierce is an incorporated city, incorporated in 1901, and the county seat of St. Lucie County. That makes it a different county from Stuart, which is the Martin County seat. It sits about 14.9 miles away, right at the edge of the range we cover, and only one of its ZIP codes, 34949, qualifies.

We say that plainly because Fort Pierce has several ZIP codes, and most are further away. If your address uses a ZIP other than 34949, call and we will confirm before booking, rather than sending someone on a long drive that cannot work.

Older and newer housing in Fort Pierce.

A city incorporated in 1901 has housing and commercial buildings from many eras. Older areas have wood and masonry structures with settled foundations and worn vents. Newer areas have block construction with different gaps. As elsewhere, the age and style of the house, more than the city name, decide where we look first.

Fort Pierce is on the coast, so humidity, salt air and vegetation are part of the picture. Roof rats use palms and hedges, and Norway rats use ground-level cover and burrows. Mice use whichever gap is open.
