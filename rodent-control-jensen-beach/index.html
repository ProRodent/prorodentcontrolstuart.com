Jensen Beach, Martin County and who to ask about local rules.

Jensen Beach is an unincorporated community, a census-designated place in Martin County with ZIP code 34957, about 4.1 miles from Stuart. It has a small piece of municipal history. It was briefly incorporated as Jensen in 1926, but the state dissolved that government in 1933, and it has been unincorporated since.

Today it is governed by the Martin County Board of County Commissioners, the same body that covers Stuart's surrounding area. The practical point is that there is no town hall to call. Questions about local rules on a property go to the county, while our coverage follows the ZIP code, 34957, and the distance from Stuart, not any municipal line.

Coastal conditions and rodents.

Jensen Beach sits on the coast side of Martin County, so humidity, salt air and dense landscaping are part of life. Those conditions keep plants fruiting and hedges thick, which feeds roof rats and offers Norway rats cover. Properties near the water edge, with mature vegetation, are the ones where we most often find outdoor burrows and rub marks along fences.

Salt air also affects the fix. Hardware that rusts in a season fails, so we choose stainless, galvanized or copper materials for sealing. A repair that works inland can fail within a year near the coast if the metal is the wrong kind.
