Hobe Sound and where it sits.

Hobe Sound is an unincorporated community, a census-designated place within Martin County, with ZIP code 33455. It lies about 8.1 miles from Stuart, the farthest of the three Martin County communities on our list, and it is governed by the county commissioners instead of a town council.

Distance matters for scheduling more than for inspection. A visit to Hobe Sound is a short drive south, and we plan it as a single trip, so it helps if you describe the problem fully on the first call.

What longer lots and quieter streets mean.

Communities further from a downtown core tend to have larger lots, more vegetation and fewer tightly packed buildings. That changes the pattern. Rodents have more yard to live in before they reach the house, so burrows, fruit trees and sheds matter more than shared walls. A visit here puts more time into the outdoor side.

Larger lots also mean more outbuildings. Sheds, detached garages, pool equipment and workshops are common, and each can hold animals that later move into the main house. We include them in the walk.

Treatment and sealing.

The sequence is the same as everywhere. Rodent trapping and removal clears the animals, and exclusion and entry point sealing closes the gaps. If a vehicle or boat is involved, our vehicle and car rodent removal page covers that part.

Because the lot is larger, a yard plan often comes with the house plan. Trimming branches back from the roof, lifting stored items off the ground and picking up fallen fruit reduce the draw.

What to expect from the first visit.

Because lots here are often larger, the walk covers more ground. We check the house, the outbuildings and the edges of the property, then plan the sequence of trapping and sealing. Expect more time outdoors than on a small-lot visit.

You hear what we found and what it would cost before any work begins. If a shed, a vehicle or a boat is part of the problem, we say which items can be handled in the same visit and which need another trip.

A return visit checks the traps and the seals. If activity continues, we widen the search along the edges of the property.
